Meet Cody Ashbury

Cody Ashbury is the pen name for co-authors Brandon Wardell and Jennifer Flaig, who met at a Midwestern community college when their offices happened to be across the hall from each other. It’s highly likely that shared interests in gum, coffee, and LEGOs are what brought them together long enough to realize they could be writing partners. They eventually collaborated on an unpublished work of nonfiction before turning their attention to a project Brandon had started years earlier with his family. With Brandon’s creative vision and Jennifer’s research and editing skills, they brought The Bed Heads: Life’s a Trip to fruition.
